# Botrem: Officially launched on BOT Chain Mainnet

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

We are thrilled to announce that **Botrem**, the premier binary options and prediction protocol, has **officially launched on BOT Chain Mainnet**! 

After a successful testnet phase and rigorous smart contract testing, our fully decentralized prediction markets are now live for everyone. Traders can now seamlessly connect their BOT Chain wallets to interact with our next-generation platform, predict BTC price movements, and earn rewards using the native BOT token.

### What is Botrem?

Botrem is a lightning-fast, decentralized binary options platform built exclusively on BOT Chain. Our protocol empowers users to predict whether the price of Bitcoin (BTC) will go UP or DOWN within a 60-second window. By leveraging BOT Chain's high-performance infrastructure, Botrem delivers an unparalleled trading experience with ultra-low latency and minimal fees.

### Key Features of Our Mainnet Launch:
* **Fully Usable Product Interface:** Our trading dashboard is live at [botrem.io](https://botrem.io). Users can connect their BOT Chain wallets, deposit stakes, and execute trades instantly.
* **On-Chain Settlement:** All trades are settled securely and transparently on the BOT Chain Mainnet (Chain ID 677).
* **Deep Liquidity Integration:** Our V2 Liquidity Vaults have been seeded and deployed on-chain, ensuring that winning trades are paid out immediately.
* **Custom Oracle Architecture:** To facilitate 60-second binary options, we deployed a custom, ultra-fast price keeper that fetches real-world BTC prices and updates the blockchain in near real-time.

### Why BOT Chain?

We chose to build Botrem on BOT Chain because of its superior EVM-compatible infrastructure, near-instant finality, and vibrant ecosystem. The ability to execute complex prediction market logic without the burden of excessive gas fees allows us to offer 60-second markets that simply wouldn't be possible elsewhere.
